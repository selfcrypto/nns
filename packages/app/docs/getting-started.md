# Getting started

The app runs in two places, and every action works in both.

- **Inside Nimiq Pay**, as a mini app. Pay hands over your addresses when you open it and signs every transaction in its own sheet.
- **In a browser**, with the Nimiq Hub as the wallet. Connect once and the Hub signs.

Both open on the landing page. Its search box takes you into Buy, **My names** opens the names you hold, and **How it works** opens these pages.

## The five tabs

Each tab is one job. Nothing is more than two taps deep.

| Tab | Job |
|---|---|
| **Buy** | Find a name, see what it resolves to, register it ([Buy](buy)) |
| **Pay** | Send NIM or USDT to a name ([Pay](pay)) |
| **My Names** | Manage the names you hold ([My Names](my-names)) |
| **Inbox** | Read and answer messages sent to your names ([Inbox](inbox)) |
| **Market** | Buy a name outright or bid in an auction ([Market](market)) |

## Your wallet

Your acting address sits in the top corner with its identicon. Tap it to see your other addresses, add one, or disconnect. Inside Nimiq Pay, disconnecting is device-local: it stops this app using your addresses, and that is all it can do.

Where a screen needs a wallet it does not have, the same control appears in place. There is one connect button in the app, not one per screen.

## Links

The screen you are on is the page's address: `#/buy/kike`, `#/names`, `#/market/indigo`, `#/docs/prices`. A reload and a shared link land where you were.

Two links carry more than a screen. A **payment link** opens Pay with the recipient and the amount filled in ([Pay](pay)). A **referral link** records who introduced you ([Referrals](referrals)). Tapping either in a chat app opens a browser rather than Nimiq Pay, so inside the mini app you paste it into the search box or the recipient field instead.

## The "?" and the "(i)"

What you must see is on the card. The reason is one tap away, behind the **?** beside a line. Inside a sheet you are about to send from, the same control is a blue **(i)**: the review shows the amount, the address and the date, and the rules behind them sit in the bubble.

## Night mode

The sun and moon button in the top corner switches it, and the choice is remembered on the device. Until you press it, the app follows the device's own setting. The landing page stays light, because its photographs were shot for a white page.

## Dates

The protocol counts blocks, not seconds. A block is roughly a second, so a date computed from a height is an estimate, and every date in the app says ≈.
